Methodology & sources

Eligible universe

S&P 500 constituents (Wikipedia) that carry weekly options (Cboe weeklys list) and trade under $120. Rebuilt on every refresh.

Downside-safety score

Composite of Fundamentals ×0.35 (leverage, liquidity, margins, ROE, FCF) · Low-vol ×0.30 (beta, realized & downside vol, worst 1-day drop, drawdown, trend) · Analyst ×0.20 · Valuation ×0.15 (P/E vs sector). Percentile-ranked within the universe.

Earnings gate

Next earnings dates cross-checked between Yahoo and the Nasdaq calendar. Names reporting on or before this week's Friday expiry are benched (red) — gap risk you don't want to be short through.

Premium estimate

Model-based, not live: the average premium of a 1-week ~0.15-delta covered call or cash-secured put (one leg — you sell a single side), priced by Black–Scholes on each name's realized volatility. For ranking income richness — confirm exact premiums in TWS before selling.

Data & refresh

Rebuilt automatically every hour during U.S. market hours (weekday ~9:35 a.m. to ~4:35 p.m. ET). Prices and premium estimates move intraday; fundamentals, analyst and valuation inputs update roughly daily. The As of stamp above shows the current data age.
